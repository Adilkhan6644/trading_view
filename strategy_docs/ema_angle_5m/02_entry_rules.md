BUY Signal Conditions (all true):

1. EMA 9 > EMA 15
2. EMA slope/angle ≥ 30° (approximation via slope/ATR)
3. Within last 3 candles, a GREEN candle touches EMA 9 or EMA 15 or both
4. The touching candle is one of: Hammer (bullish), Bullish Marubozu, or strong bullish candle with small wicks
5. Show BUY label above the candle on main chart

SELL Signal Conditions (all true):

1. EMA 15 > EMA 9
2. EMA slope/angle ≥ 30°
3. Within last 3 candles, a RED candle touches EMA 9 or EMA 15 or both
4. The touching candle is one of: strong bearish candle with small wicks, Bearish Marubozu, Inverted hammer red candle
5. Show SELL label above the candle on main chart

BB / SS Rules:
- "BB": first green candle touching EMA when EMA9>EMA15 and angle≥30° (show "BB" above candle)
- "SS": first red candle touching EMA when EMA15>EMA9 and angle≥30° (show "SS" above candle)

Notes:
- Avoid duplicates and repainting (use candle-close confirmation when possible).
- Add ADX/ATR filters to reduce false signals.
