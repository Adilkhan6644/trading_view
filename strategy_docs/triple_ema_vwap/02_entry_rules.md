Entry Rules

LONG
- EMA 8 crosses above EMA 21 (cross on current or previous candle)
- Price is above EMA 55
- Price is above VWAP
- Volume above recent rolling average (optional filter)

SHORT
- EMA 8 crosses below EMA 21
- Price is below EMA 55
- Price is below VWAP
- Volume above recent rolling average (optional filter)

Notes
- Confirm entries on candle close for non-repainting behavior when possible.
- Optionally require EMA 8 slope > threshold to reduce choppy entries.
