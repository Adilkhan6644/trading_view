⚡ Strategy #1: Triple EMA + VWAP — Logic

- EMAs: short=8, medium=21, trend=55.
- VWAP is used as the institutional fair-value anchor; direction is biased by price relative to VWAP.
- Longs only when price > EMA55 and price > VWAP; shorts only when price < EMA55 and price < VWAP.
- EMA 8/21 cross produces signal candidates; confirmation via price vs EMA55 and VWAP reduces false entries.
- Use volume and momentum filters (e.g., EMA slope or ATR) to avoid weak moves.
- Intended for intraday scalping (1m–5m) on high-liquidity pairs (BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT).
